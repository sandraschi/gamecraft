# Where is Benny?: cheat sheet

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Rounds | 3 (130/24, 200/34, 270/44 people/dogs) |
| Look-alikes | 5 German Shepherds + 1 Malinois |
| Neckwear | red, blue or yellow bandana; red, green or purple collar |
| Carried | tennis ball, stick or orange frisbee |
| Hint | after 30 s, names the nearest stall or the carousel |

Controls: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, click a dog.
The Malinois has no black saddle on its back.
