# Where is Benny?

Hidden-object game at a sunny funfair: six striped stalls, a turning carousel, a
Ferris wheel, bunting, and a crowd of 130 / 200 / 270 instanced people with
24 / 34 / 44 dogs over three rounds. The poster (with its own turning 3D portrait)
says what Benny wears and carries today. Five other German Shepherds and one
Malinois each get at least one detail wrong, and say so when clicked.

Orbit to look around, click a dog to check it. After 30 s a hint names the stall
he is near.

Self-test (60 fairs): exactly one dog matches the poster, 4+ shepherd look-alikes of
which 3+ differ by exactly one detail, the Malinois never counts, every wrong dog
gets an explanation, nobody stands inside a stall, 12+ different outfits.
Tour: the bot swings the camera until nothing blocks the dog, clicks a look-alike
first, then finds Benny in all three rounds.
