Bento

Everything on the mat has to go into the box, with no gaps and nothing on top of anything else.

Click a piece to pick it up. Turn it with R, right-click or the scroll wheel. Hover over the box: green means it fits, red means it does not. Click to set it down; click a packed piece to take it out again.

Every box was cut from a full one, so there is always an answer. Six box sizes, from a child's lunch to a jubako tier.
