# Gassi

Benny walks a route once through a stylized Alsergrund (5x5 street grid with real
street names, not a map; Strudlhofstiege, Palais Liechtenstein, Berggasse 19,
Sigmund-Freud-Park, Votivkirche, Volksoper, Franz-Josefs-Bahnhof). The glowing trail
fades; you lead him back along it by clicking corners or using arrow keys. Walks
of 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 blocks. A wrong turn earns a look; after two at the same
corner he sniffs toward the right street.

Self-test (5 lengths x 40 routes): simple paths of the right length starting at a
landmark, with turns, every step one block, and blind guessing under 3% from six
blocks on (measured 1.6%). Tour: watches the demo, takes one wrong turn, then walks
two routes by clicking.

Uses Benny from `../where-is-benny/dogs.js`.
