Gassi

Benny knows his walks. He shows you the way once, leaving a glowing trail; then the trail fades and it is your turn to lead.

Click the next corner (the glowing circles) or use the arrow keys. A wrong turn costs nothing: Benny just gives you a look, and after two tries he sniffs in the right direction.

The streets carry Alsergrund names, but the grid is stylized, not a map. Walks get longer as you go.
