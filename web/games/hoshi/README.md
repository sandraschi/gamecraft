# Hoshi

A sky built from real star positions (approximate J2000 RA/Dec and magnitudes) for
48 named stars, plus 2400 seeded background stars kept clear of them and a faint
Milky Way band. Seven figures with Western and Japanese names and a line of lore:
Big Dipper (hokuto shichisei), Cassiopeia (yamagata-boshi), Orion (tsuzumi-boshi),
Vega and Altair (Orihime and Hikoboshi), Cygnus, the Pleiades (Subaru) and
Scorpius (tsuri-boshi).

Click a star, then another; lines in the figure turn gold and stay, others fade.

Self-test: every line uses real stars, figures are connected, no duplicate lines,
each figure fits its view at 1280x720, and no two of its stars are within
26 px on screen. Tour: a wrong pair first, then three constellations drawn by
clicking projected star positions.
