Hoshi

A clear night. The card on the left shows a constellation's stick figure, with its Western and Japanese names. The stars are real: these are their actual positions in the sky.

Click a star, then another, to draw a line between them. Lines that belong to the figure turn gold and stay; others fade. Bigger dots on the card are brighter stars.

Drag to look around, scroll to zoom. "Find it" turns you back toward the figure. No timer.

The whole sky

Seven figures, from the Big Dipper to the fish-hook of Scorpius. The lines you drew stay in the sky; drag around to see them.
