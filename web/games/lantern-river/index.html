Lantern River

On summer nights, paper lanterns are set on the river to carry light downstream. Tonight the little shrine on the far bank would like some of them to stay.

Hold the pointer (or a finger) on the water and the lanterns nearby drift away from it, as if you breathed on them. Ease them into the cove under the red gate.

There is no hurry and nothing to lose: lanterns you miss float on to the sea. Every two that arrive light a stone lantern; twelve light the gate.
