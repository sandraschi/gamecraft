Orgelbauer

The little organ lost its pipes. When you press Play, it sounds its pipes from left to right. Make it play the tune.

Listen to the tune as often as you like. Click a pipe to hear it and pick it up, then click a hole to stand it there. Click a placed pipe to take it out again; putting a pipe on an occupied hole swaps them.

Like on a real organ, a longer pipe sounds lower. After playing, green tags are right notes and red ones are wrong. Every tune is traditional.

It plays!
