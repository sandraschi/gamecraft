Kage-e

Someone hung a heap of junk blocks in front of a lamp. From exactly one direction, its shadow on the wall becomes a picture. The name of the picture is at the top, in Japanese; the meter shows how close the shadow is.

Drag left and right to spin it, up and down to tip it. Arrow keys nudge.

Peek draws the picture's outline on the wall if you want it. No timer, nothing to lose.
