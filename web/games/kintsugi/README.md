# Kintsugi

Four bowls (chawan, donburi, hachi, yunomi), each broken along seeded jagged
cracks into 4 to 6 wall shards around a foot that stays in place. Shards are real
shell geometry (outside, inside, rim and crack faces). Turn (15 degree steps), flip
and drag each home; when the last one settles, gold tubes grow along every crack
and the foot ring, and the bowl turns slowly.

Self-test (80 fractures): cracks never cross (every shard keeps at least 0.35 rad),
no shard starts solved, scatter stays on the cloth and 1.4 m apart; the snap rule
accepts the true pose and rejects a 2-step turn, a flip, or 0.5 m off.
Tour: carries a shard home unturned first (must not snap), then mends the bowl.
