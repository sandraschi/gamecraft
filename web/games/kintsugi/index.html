Kintsugi

A bowl fell. Its foot is still standing in the middle of the cloth; the shards lie around it.

Drag a shard toward its place. Turn it (Q / E, scroll, or the buttons) and flip it (F) until its edges match. When it fits, it settles home.

When the last shard is home, the cracks fill with gold. Kintsugi is the Japanese art of mending with gold: the break becomes part of the bowl's history rather than something to hide.
