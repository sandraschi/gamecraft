# Spot the Difference: cheat sheet

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Differences per round | 5 |
| Kinds | colour, hidden, moved, turned (22 to 46 deg), grown x1.4 to x1.45, clock hands |
| Click slack | 10 px around a difference |
| Hint | after 20 s without a find |
| Penalty for misses | none, just counted |

Look first at the shelves (at most one book per round), the picture frames
(they tilt or change colour), and the floor clutter (yarn, slippers, magazines, cat).
