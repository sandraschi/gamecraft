# Spot the Difference

One cluttered living room (`../_shared/room.js`, procedural, deterministic) rendered
twice side by side; the right copy gets five changes: colour, missing, moved, turned,
grown, or a clock showing another time. Differences are drawn fresh every round
from the room's ~87 items, so there is no answer key to memorise.

- Click either picture. A hit puts gold rings on both; a miss shows a small cross.
- Moved or grown things count where they are now as well as where they were.
- Hint (after 20 s without a find) circles the area of one difference.

Self-test (60 seeds): always five, all distinct items, none tiny on screen, no two
overlapping, every change really alters the item, unchanged copies are identical,
50+ distinct difference sets. Tour: one deliberate wrong click, five finds, a new
round with a different set.

Shares the room with [Tidy the Room](../tidy-the-room/).
