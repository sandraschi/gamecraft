Hnefatafl

Copenhagen rules on 11x11. The defenders (ivory, with the king) win when the king reaches a corner. The attackers (red) win by capturing the king. Attackers move first.

  • Every piece moves like a rook: any distance in a line, no jumping.
  • Only the king may stop on the throne (centre) or a corner. Other pieces may pass over the empty throne.
  • Capture a piece by closing a line around it: your piece, the enemy, your piece. Moving between two enemies is safe. Corners, and the throne while empty, count as hostile to everyone. The king fights too.
  • The king is taken by attackers on all four sides, or on three sides when he stands next to the empty throne. On the board edge he cannot be taken.
  • A side with no legal move loses. Three repetitions of a position is a draw.

Also implemented: shieldwall capture (a row of pieces on an edge, both ends hostile and an enemy in front of each piece, is taken together; never the king), the edge-fort win (the king on an edge with a free move and two or more defenders that can never be captured; the test is cautious and may miss an exotic fort, never invents one) and the encirclement win (an unbroken ring of attackers, diagonal links allowed, around the king and every defender). The opponent is a small fixed-depth alpha-beta search; "easy" sometimes plays a random move.
