    The Quiet Well

    The light rises out of the well and every lantern in Mossbrook catches at once.

    Next Lantern Eve the whole village climbs the crag with lanterns lit from every hearth. Bertil carries nothing heavier than a cake. Old Knuckles listens to the song with his eyes closed, and afterwards Odo is asked, firmly and by everyone, not to sing next year.
