# Mossbrook cheat sheet

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | move (drag the left half of the screen on touch) |
| Shift | run |
| E / Enter | talk, use, read; again to continue |
| Q | cycle lantern flame |
| J | journal |
| T | cycle time of day (or 1 dawn, 2 noon, 3 dusk, 4 night) |
| N / M | sound / music |
| wheel | zoom |
| H | help |
| Esc | close dialogue or help |

`?autoplay=1` on the URL plays the whole story by itself.

## The lantern

| Flame | Get it | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | you start with it (any hearth) | the root curtain in the cave |
| Glimmer | the crates in the forge cellar | the glyph in the tunnel wall (only visible with Glimmer up) |
| Hush | the Moonring altar, south of the village | walking past the sleeping Keeper |

## Stealth near the Keeper

The stir meter fills while you are in the cave within about 16 m of him.

| You are | Thrown out after |
|---|---|
| Ember, walking | about 2.7 s |
| Ember, running | about 1.7 s |
| Hush, walking | never |
| Hush, running | about 8.3 s |

Being thrown out is not game over: you land at the cave mouth and can try again.

## Map

- Village green and the well: the middle.
- Copper Kettle (tavern): north of the green. Stairs up on the west side, cellar
  stairs in the north-east corner.
- Lantern Archive: east of the tavern, door on the south side.
- Smithy: east of the green. Cellar stairs along the north wall.
- Bakery: south-west, door on its south side (go round via the south road).
- Knuckle Crag and the cave: follow the road west.
- River, bridge and Old Wenzel: follow the road east from the Archive.
- Moonring: south road past the bakery, up into the hills.

## Walkthrough (spoilers)

1. **Mags** (behind the bar, Copper Kettle) tells you the well went dark.
2. **Sister Wren** (Archive): the well is "the Keeper's Cradle" in a cellar the
   village does not admit to; pages about a compact are torn out.
3. **Tavern cellar, then the tunnel south:** read the damp note ("Bertil knows").
4. **Forge cellar** (end of the tunnel, east): search the crates. Crystal under
   the coal. You learn **Glimmer**.
5. **Back in the tunnel, just past the junction under the well:** switch to
   Glimmer (Q). A glyph appears on the north wall. Use it; the wall opens.
6. **The Keeper's Cellar:** examine the brass cradle. Nearly empty, and
   Bertil's tools are next to it.
7. **Bertil** (smithy, upstairs from his cellar). Twist one: he has been
   *carrying* crystal to keep the well alive for six years. The heart crystals
   grow behind the troll; Hush comes from the Moonring.
8. **Moonring** (far south): stand at the altar. You learn **Hush**.
9. **Cave:** Hush on, walk (do not run) through the hall to the roots at its
   south end. The Keeper sleeps in the north part; the stir meter fills anywhere
   within about 16 m of him. Switch to **Ember** and burn them.
10. **The Keeper's Hollow:** read the tablet. Twist two: the village promised the
    Keeper a lantern lit from every hearth and a song each Lantern Eve, and
    forgot for forty years. He is not a thief; he is owed.
11. **Three hearths:** light the lantern at the Copper Kettle fireplace, Bertil's
    forge, and Pim's bakery oven (any order).
12. **Wake the Keeper:** Hush on, walk up to him, use him. Odo sings. Badly.
    He gives you the heart crystal.
13. **Back to the Keeper's Cellar:** set the heart crystal in the cradle. The well
    is lit; the ending plays. You can keep wandering afterwards.

## Optional

- **Old Wenzel** by the river saw blue lights crossing the bridge at night.
- **Corwin** (armchair by the tavern fire) half-remembers the song.
- **Ysolde** (upstairs in the tavern) noticed a draught from under the green.
- **Captain Osk** (west road) changes his tune after the tablet.
- Villagers' lines change at the end; it is worth one more lap of the village.
