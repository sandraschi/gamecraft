# Tidy the Room

The Spot the Difference room after a lively afternoon: six movable things lie on
the floor. A photo in the corner (a second render of the tidy room) shows where
they belong. Drag each home; it snaps within 0.22 m and otherwise stays where it
is dropped. No timer pressure.

Self-test (80 seeds): always six, never placed within 3x the snap distance of home,
on open floor, at least 0.5 m apart, inside a 4:3 view and clear of the photo
corner, 40+ distinct messes. Tour: one deliberate wrong drop that must stay
untidy, then all six home, the room must match the photo exactly, then a new mess.
