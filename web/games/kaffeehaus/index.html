Café Morgenstern

You are the Herr Ober. Guests wait at the door; you decide where they sit and in what order things get done.

Each guest shows buttons. Seat (then click a table), Serve when their order is ready, Bill when they ask to pay, Newspaper if they would like one. Seating includes taking the order. Jobs queue up on the left; reorder or cancel them there.

The ring next to a name is their patience. Green is fine, red means they are about to give up.

Regulars (gold border) have a favourite table and a usual order. Treat them well and they come back every day. Rush bill frees a table early, but nobody likes being rushed, and a guest left in peace often orders a second round.

Space pauses. 1, 2, 3 set the speed. S toggles sound, M the waltz. At closing you see the day's takings, how your regulars felt, and can buy upgrades.
