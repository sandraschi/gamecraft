# Toy Railway: cheat sheet

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Zoom | Up/Down or W/S (hold): whole table down to eye height on a platform |
| Look | Left/Right or A/D: orbit the table, or look around on the platform |
| Platform | Tab cycles Talheim 1, Talheim 2, Bergblick 1, Bergblick 2 |
| Follow | F jumps to the platform with the next train due and zooms in |
| Speed | + and - (1x to 64x), Space pauses |
| Day and night | N shifts the light by half a toy day (a toy day is 12 minutes) |
| Back | Home returns to the whole table |
| Trains | Sparky (steam tank, 14 m/s, stops in the passing loops), Bullet (40 m/s, overtakes in the stations) |
| Boards | Track number, then minutes:seconds to the next scheduled arrival |
| Gate | Launch with --gate: a whole railway day runs headless first |
| Win line | GameCraft: railway ran safe and on time |
